# Chart Reading Techniques: The Three Harmonies and Four Cardinals

---

The single most common mistake beginners make in Zi Wei Dou Shu is **reading palaces in isolation**. They see Wu Qu (武曲) in the Wealth Palace and conclude they'll be rich. They spot Tan Lang (貪狼) in the Spouse Palace and expect a fairy-tale romance. Then reality delivers a sharp correction — because isolated readings miss the full picture.

The **Three Harmonies and Four Cardinals (三方四正, San Fang Si Zheng)** is the first essential technique for moving from point-based reading to **three-dimensional chart analysis**. Master this, and you'll understand why ZWDS practitioners say: *"A lone star tells no story; a lone palace renders no verdict."*

## Why Single-Palace Reading Fails

Imagine a doctor whose patient complains of a cough. A lazy doctor prescribes cough syrup and sends them home. A good doctor orders a chest X-ray, checks for fever, asks about recent exposures, and considers whether the cough indicates cold, pneumonia, or something more serious.

A single cough (one palace) could mean anything. Only by examining the surrounding systems (other palaces) can you reach an accurate diagnosis. In ZWDS, the San Fang Si Zheng framework tells you exactly **which surrounding systems to examine**.

## The Structure of San Fang Si Zheng

For any palace you're analyzing (the "target palace"), the San Fang Si Zheng consists of:

### The Three Harmonies (三方) — 120-Degree Triangle

Three palaces form a triangle around your twelve-palace chart, each separated by 120 degrees. These are the palaces that **share energy** with your target palace — their stars directly influence how the target palace manifests.

### The Opposite Palace (對宮) — 180-Degree Opposition

The palace directly across from your target creates a **tension-and-complement relationship**. The opposite palace's stars exert powerful influence — sometimes as strong as stars actually sitting in the target palace.

### Combined: The Four Cardinals (四正)

Together, the three triangle palaces plus the opposite palace give you four positions to examine for any single palace analysis. This is the **minimum scope** for any serious reading.

## The Life Palace's San Fang Si Zheng: The Core Identity Network

The most important San Fang Si Zheng configuration centers on the **Life Palace (命宮)**:

- **Life Palace** — your core self
- **Career Palace (官祿宮)** — how you express yourself through work
- **Wealth Palace (財帛宮)** — how you relate to material resources
- **Travel Palace (遷移宮)** — how the external world receives you

These four palaces form your **fundamental life equation**. They must be read together:

### Reading the Network

**Example 1:** Life Palace has Zi Wei (紫微) — natural leadership. Career Palace has Qi Sha (七殺) — aggressive, pioneering career energy. Wealth Palace has Tian Fu (天府) — conservative financial management. Travel Palace has Tian Xiang (天相) — well-received publicly.

**Interpretation:** A strong leader who pursues ambitious career goals but manages money conservatively. The external world sees them as trustworthy and orderly. All four palaces support each other — this is a highly functional configuration.

**Example 2:** Life Palace has Tian Ji (天機) — analytical and cautious. Career Palace has Tai Yang (太陽) — visible, public-facing work. Wealth Palace has Ju Men (巨門) — money through expertise and speaking. Travel Palace has Tian Liang (天梁) — perceived as wise and principled externally.

**Interpretation:** An intellectual who earns through professional expertise and public speaking. The analytical nature supports the knowledge-based career, and the external world values their wisdom. Coherent network — strong potential for consulting, education, or advisory roles.

**Example 3:** Life Palace has Wu Qu (武曲) — decisive and financially focused. Career Palace has Po Jun (破軍) — constantly changing professional direction. Wealth Palace has Lian Zhen (廉貞) — emotionally complex relationship with money. Travel Palace has Tan Lang (貪狼) — charismatic but desire-driven external image.

**Interpretation:** A financially capable person whose career lacks stability due to Po Jun's revolutionary energy. Money comes and goes through emotionally charged channels. The external world is attracted by their charisma but may not take them seriously. This network has internal contradictions — career instability undermines financial discipline. The person needs to find a career that channels Po Jun's disruption constructively.

## Other Important San Fang Si Zheng Configurations

### The Spouse Palace Network

- **Spouse Palace** — intimate relationship patterns
- **Career Palace** — how work affects marriage
- **Fortune Palace (福德宮)** — whether the relationship nourishes your inner life
- **Travel Palace** — external social influences on the relationship

### The Wealth Palace Network

- **Wealth Palace** — financial management style
- **Life Palace** — personality driving financial decisions
- **Career Palace** — professional earning capacity
- **Fortune Palace** — whether money brings genuine satisfaction

## Advanced Technique: Borrowing Stars

When the opposite palace contains stars but your target palace is empty, those opposite stars **lend their influence** across the axis. This is called "borrowing stars (借星)" and is crucial for reading empty palaces:

- An empty Life Palace borrows from the Travel Palace — your external persona heavily shapes your self-identity
- An empty Wealth Palace borrows from the Fortune Palace — your inner values drive your financial behavior
- An empty Career Palace borrows from the Spouse Palace — relationships significantly influence your professional direction

## Practical Application Steps

When analyzing any life question:

1. **Identify the primary palace** — which palace directly governs this question?
2. **Map its San Fang Si Zheng** — identify the three triangle palaces and the opposite palace
3. **Read the primary palace** — note its stars, transformers, and brightness grades
4. **Read each supporting palace** — note how their stars reinforce or contradict the primary
5. **Synthesize** — the true answer emerges from the interaction of all four palaces, not from any single one

---

San Fang Si Zheng transforms ZWDS from a collection of isolated observations into a **coherent analytical system**. Once you learn to read palaces as networks rather than individual units, chart interpretation becomes simultaneously more nuanced and more reliable. This technique is the gateway from beginner to intermediate ZWDS practice — and the foundation upon which all advanced techniques build.
