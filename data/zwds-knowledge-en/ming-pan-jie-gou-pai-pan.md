# Chart Structure and Chart Construction in Zi Wei Dou Shu

---

Before interpreting a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart, you need to understand how one is built. The chart construction process (排盤, Pai Pan) transforms birth data into a complete destiny map through a precise mathematical procedure. This guide explains the structure of the chart and the logic behind its construction.

## The Chart Grid: Twelve Palaces in a Square

A ZWDS chart is arranged as a **4×4 square grid** with the center left empty, creating twelve outer positions — one for each palace. Unlike Western astrology's circular wheel, the ZWDS chart is rectilinear, with palaces arranged in fixed positions around the perimeter.

### The Twelve Palaces (十二宮)

Each palace governs a specific life domain:

| Palace | Chinese | Domain |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Life Palace | 命宮 | Core personality, life direction |
| Siblings Palace | 兄弟宮 | Sibling relationships, close peers |
| Spouse Palace | 夫妻宮 | Marriage, romantic partnerships |
| Children Palace | 子女宮 | Offspring, creativity |
| Wealth Palace | 財帛宮 | Financial patterns, earning style |
| Health Palace | 疾厄宮 | Physical constitution, vulnerabilities |
| Travel Palace | 遷移宮 | External environment, public image |
| Friends Palace | 交友宮 | Social networks, subordinates |
| Career Palace | 官祿宮 | Professional destiny, ambition |
| Property Palace | 田宅宮 | Real estate, family assets |
| Fortune Palace | 福德宮 | Inner happiness, spiritual life |
| Parents Palace | 父母宮 | Family background, authority figures |

### Palace Arrangement Logic

Palaces always appear in the same **counter-clockwise sequence** around the grid. What changes between different charts is **where the Life Palace falls** — this starting position shifts based on birth month and birth hour, pulling all other palaces along with it.

## The Construction Process

### Required Birth Data

ZWDS chart construction requires four data points:
1. **Birth year** — determines the Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch of the year
2. **Birth month** (lunar calendar) — determines palace positions
3. **Birth day** (lunar calendar) — determines the Five Elements Bureau (五行局)
4. **Birth hour** (two-hour period) — refines the Life Palace position

### Step 1: Determine the Life Palace Position

Using the birth month and birth hour, locate the Life Palace on the grid. The formula follows a specific lookup table that cross-references month and hour to produce a grid position (Earthly Branch).

**Key insight:** This is why birth hour accuracy matters so much in ZWDS. A difference of one two-hour period shifts the entire chart — every palace moves, every star changes position.

### Step 2: Assign All Twelve Palaces

Starting from the Life Palace position, assign the remaining eleven palaces in counter-clockwise order. Each person's palace arrangement is unique to their birth data.

### Step 3: Determine the Five Elements Bureau (五行局)

The combination of Life Palace position and birth year Heavenly Stem determines your **Five Elements Bureau** — a number (2, 3, 4, 5, or 6) that governs how the primary star Zi Wei (紫微) gets placed on the chart. The five bureaus are:

- Water Bureau (水二局) — value 2
- Wood Bureau (木三局) — value 3
- Metal Bureau (金四局) — value 4
- Earth Bureau (土五局) — value 5
- Fire Bureau (火六局) — value 6

### Step 4: Place the Zi Wei Star

Using the Five Elements Bureau number and the birth day, calculate Zi Wei's position through a specific formula. Once Zi Wei is placed, all six stars in the **Zi Wei cluster** follow fixed positional relationships — they automatically fill their designated spots relative to Zi Wei.

### Step 5: Place the Tian Fu Star

Tian Fu's position mirrors Zi Wei's through a symmetry rule. Once Tian Fu is placed, the remaining eight stars of the **Tian Fu cluster** automatically occupy their fixed positions relative to Tian Fu.

### Step 6: Place Auxiliary and Minor Stars

Additional stars — auspicious stars, challenging stars, and minor influences — are placed according to their own rules, typically derived from:
- Birth year Heavenly Stem or Earthly Branch
- Birth month
- Birth hour
- Birth day
- Life Palace position

### Step 7: Assign the Four Transformers (四化)

The birth year's Heavenly Stem determines which four primary stars receive the Lu (祿), Quan (權), Ke (科), and Ji (忌) transformations. This is done through a fixed lookup table — each of the ten Heavenly Stems activates a specific transformer set.

### Step 8: Determine the Body Palace (身宮)

The Body Palace shares a position with one of six specific palaces, determined by birth hour. Its placement adds an additional layer of interpretation to whichever palace it co-occupies.

## Chart Structure Concepts

### Brightness Grades (廟旺利陷)

Each primary star has preferred positions on the grid where it shines brightest and positions where it's dimmed:

- **廟 (Temple):** Maximum power — the star expresses its best qualities
- **旺 (Prosperous):** Strong power — nearly full expression
- **利 (Beneficial):** Moderate power — functional but not dominant
- **陷 (Fallen):** Minimum power — the star struggles to express positively

The same star can produce completely different personality effects depending on its brightness grade — a fallen Zi Wei is vastly different from a temple-grade Zi Wei.

### Palace Relationships

Beyond San Fang Si Zheng, palaces relate through several geometric patterns:

- **Opposite palaces (對宮):** 180° apart — tension and complementarity
- **Triangle palaces (三合):** 120° apart — mutual support
- **Adjacent palaces:** Side by side — indirect influence

### The Twelve Earthly Branch Positions

Each grid position corresponds to an Earthly Branch with specific elemental properties. Stars placed in positions whose elements support their own nature perform well; stars in conflicting-element positions face friction.

## Modern Chart Construction

Today, ZWDS charts are typically generated by software — the mathematical calculations that once took skilled practitioners significant time are now computed instantly. However, understanding the construction process remains valuable because:

1. **It reveals why charts differ** — small changes in birth data produce very different charts
2. **It explains star behavior** — brightness grades and elemental interactions make more sense when you understand placement logic
3. **It enables error checking** — knowing the rules helps you verify software accuracy
4. **It deepens interpretation** — understanding why a star landed where it did enriches your reading of what it means

---

Chart construction is the engineering behind ZWDS's artistry. While you don't need to calculate charts by hand in the modern era, understanding the structure — twelve palaces, two star clusters, brightness grades, and temporal layers — gives you the architectural knowledge needed to read any chart with confidence and depth.
